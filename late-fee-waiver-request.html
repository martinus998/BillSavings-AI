Late fee waiver request script

A late fee may sometimes be reviewed when the account otherwise has a good payment history or there was a one-time problem. Ask clearly and politely without assuming a waiver is guaranteed.

Build your waiver request

Enter the details you know, then generate the request.

Runs in your browser. Do not enter account numbers, passwords or payment-card details.

Ask, do not demand

Explain that you are requesting a one-time courtesy review and ask whether the provider can remove or reduce the fee. A calm factual request is usually clearer than a long explanation.

Confirm future payment settings

If autopay, paperless billing or a saved payment method contributed to the problem, verify the setting before ending the call so the same issue does not repeat.

If the fee appears incorrect

If you believe the late fee was charged in error rather than simply asking for a courtesy waiver, use the billing error dispute template.

Frequently asked questions

Can I ask a provider to waive a late fee?

Yes. You can ask whether a one-time courtesy waiver or reduction is available, but approval depends on the provider and account history.

What should I say?

State the fee, briefly explain the one-time issue, confirm that the balance is being addressed and ask whether a courtesy waiver is available.

Should I claim the fee is wrong if it is not?

No. If you are asking for a courtesy waiver, say that directly rather than presenting it as a billing error.
