# ExpenseLeak AI — next project plan

Status: planning only. This file documents the next standalone project; it is not part of BillSavings AI production functionality.

Product: AI business expense auditor for U.S. small businesses.
Core job: upload statements/invoices/CSV, identify recurring SaaS/vendor charges, duplicates, price increases, renewal risks, and possible savings opportunities.
Positioning: informational expense review, not accounting/tax/legal advice.
Suggested pricing: Free preview; one-time audit $29; Pro monitoring $29/month after recurring monitoring is implemented.
Suggested brand/domain: ExpenseLeak AI / expenseleakai.com (domain availability should be rechecked at purchase time).

Launch guardrails:
- Never claim guaranteed savings.
- Do not sell recurring monitoring until it exists.
- Do not claim bank-grade security, SOC 2, or encryption guarantees unless verified.
- Do not accept medical data.
- Before payments, implement secure upload, auth, deletion policy, Stripe checkout, webhook entitlement, cancellation path, privacy/terms/refund/security pages, and analytics.
