Review the bill before the call

Mark the base package, broadcast or regional charges, equipment, premium channels, add-ons and expired discounts. If the total rose unexpectedly, use the cable bill increase guide.

Ask for the all-in price

If the provider offers a new package, ask what the bill will be after equipment, recurring fees and the promotion are included. Also ask when the promotion expires.

Remove what you do not use

Premium channels, extra boxes and bundled services can add recurring cost. Compare the savings from removing them against any bundle discount they support.

Frequently asked questions

Can I negotiate a cable bill after a promotion ends?

You can ask the provider to review current promotions, lower-cost packages, equipment options and available discounts. Eligibility varies by account.

Should I cancel channels before calling?

It can help to know which services you actually use, but ask how removing them changes the full package price and discounts.

What price should I compare?

Compare the full recurring monthly total and any one-time fees, not just the advertised base package.
