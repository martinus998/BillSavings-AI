Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers
What can BillSavings AI analyze?

Supported consumer bills and statements such as utilities, internet, mobile services, subscriptions and other non-medical documents.

Does BillSavings AI guarantee savings?

No. Results are informational estimates. Actual savings depend on provider terms, eligibility and the actions you take.

How can it help lower monthly bills?

It can highlight recurring charges, possible fees and other line items worth reviewing, then present practical next steps to consider.

Is this financial advice?

No. BillSavings AI provides informational bill-review results and estimates, not financial, legal or tax advice.
