Questions

Frequently asked questions.

Are payments active this weekend?

No. The public beta is for testing the experience. Paid checkout is scheduled to open Monday.

Does the weekend beta upload my document?

No. In this preview, the selected file stays in your browser and is not transmitted by this page.

Is BillSavings AI financial, legal or medical advice?

No. It is a software tool intended to help organize billing information and identify items for further review. Important decisions should be verified with the relevant provider or qualified professional.

Will every review find savings?

No. Results will depend on the document and situation, and no savings amount is guaranteed.
