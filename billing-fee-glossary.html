BILLING FEE GLOSSARY

Common bill charges explained in plain English

Use this glossary when a fee label on your statement is unclear. Start with the exact wording on the bill, then compare older statements and the provider's current terms before deciding whether the charge is expected, optional or worth disputing.

How to use this glossary

The same label can mean different things at different companies. Treat each definition as a starting point, then open the detailed guide and compare the charge against your own provider documents.

Administrative fee

A broad provider-created account or service fee. It is not automatically a government tax just because it appears next to taxes.

Administrative fee guide →

Activation fee

A one-time charge that can appear when a new line, device or service is started or changed.

Phone activation fee guide →

Autopay discount

A recurring credit that may depend on using an eligible payment method and keeping automatic payments active.

Lost autopay discount guide →

Convenience fee

A charge tied to a particular payment method or payment channel. It is different from a credit-card convenience check.

Convenience fee guide →

Device upgrade fee

A one-time carrier charge associated with upgrading or changing a financed device.

Device upgrade fee guide →

Early termination fee

A charge that may apply when ending a service agreement before the contract term is complete.

Early termination fee guide →

Equipment rental fee

A recurring charge for a modem, router, gateway, receiver or other provider-owned equipment.

Equipment rental fee guide →

Late fee

A penalty charged after payment is not received by the provider's due date.

Late fee guide → · Waiver request →

Paper statement fee

A charge for receiving a printed statement instead of electronic billing.

Paper statement fee guide →

Payment processing fee

A fee connected to processing a payment, sometimes tied to a payment method or assisted-payment channel.

Payment processing fee guide →

Prorated charge

A partial-period charge after service starts, stops or changes in the middle of a billing cycle.

Prorated charge guide →

Reconnection fee

A charge for restoring service after suspension or disconnection.

Reconnection fee guide →

Regulatory recovery fee

A provider-imposed charge intended to recover some regulatory-related business costs. It should not automatically be described as a government tax.

Regulatory recovery fee guide →

Returned payment fee

A charge after a bank transfer, card or other payment is returned or rejected.

Returned payment fee guide →

Roaming charge

A usage charge that can appear when a mobile device uses another network or service area under the carrier's terms.

Roaming charge guide →

Service charge

A generic fee label that needs context from the provider because it can cover many different account or service costs.

Service charge guide →

If the charge still does not make sense

Compare the current bill with an older statement, find the first month the charge appeared, and ask the provider to explain the fee in writing. If you believe it is an error, use the billing error dispute template.

Definitions are general educational explanations. Provider terms and local rules vary. BillSavings AI does not decide that a charge is invalid.
