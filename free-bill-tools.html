Which tool should I use first?

  • My bill went up: start with the Monthly Bill Change Calculator.
  • I may pay a negotiation service: use the Negotiation Fee Calculator before enrolling.
  • I want to call the provider myself: use the Script Generator.
  • I do not know what changed: start with the AI Bill Checker or the guide hub.
  • I see an unfamiliar fee: use the Hidden Fee Checklist and then the matching fee guide.

Why the tools are separate

A bill increase, a negotiation-service fee and a provider call are different problems. Separating them keeps the math transparent and makes it easier to verify each step against the actual bill and provider terms.

These tools do not decide that a charge is wrong and do not guarantee a lower bill. They are designed to help you ask better questions and compare recurring costs more clearly.

Privacy note: the calculators and script generator above run in your browser. Do not enter account numbers, passwords, PINs or other sensitive credentials into free planning fields.
