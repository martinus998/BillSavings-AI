Reconnection fee on your bill? Check the timeline

A reconnection or restoration charge may appear after service was interrupted. The key is matching the fee to the interruption, payment and restoration dates.

3. Payment posted

4. Service restored

Note the date and time service became active again.

Other charges may appear at the same time

A reconnection fee can appear with a late fee, returned-payment fee, past-due balance or deposit-related item. Review each line separately rather than treating the entire final amount as one fee.

If the account also shows a late fee, use our late fee checklist. If a payment failed or was reversed, see our returned payment fee guide.

Questions to ask the provider

  • What event triggered the reconnection fee?
  • What payment date did you record?
  • Was restoration automatic or manually processed?
  • Is the charge recurring or one-time?
  • Are there any additional restoration or deposit charges still pending?

Frequently asked questions

Can a provider charge both a late fee and reconnection fee?

Policies vary, and the charges can be separate under some provider terms.

Can the fee be removed?

That depends on the provider's rules and account history. Ask what policy applies and whether an adjustment is available.

Does BillSavings AI restore service?

No. It helps organize billing information so you can understand the charge and contact the provider.
