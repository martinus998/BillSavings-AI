Your subscription price increased. What should you check first?

Do not compare only the new total. Check the old price, billing cycle, plan tier, add-ons, taxes, discounts and renewal date so you know exactly what changed before you cancel or downgrade.

Review My Recurring Charges

Six reasons a recurring subscription can cost more

Standard price increase

The provider may have raised the regular price for the same plan.

Promotion ended

An introductory or retention discount may have expired at renewal.

Plan tier changed

Your account may now include a higher tier, more users or additional features.

Billing frequency changed

Monthly and annual charges can be easy to compare incorrectly if the cycle changed.

Tax or regional pricing

Taxes and location-based pricing can change the final amount even when the base subscription is similar.

Added service

An optional add-on, extra seat, storage upgrade or premium feature may have been added.

Compare the subscription on an annual basis

A small monthly increase becomes more meaningful when multiplied across a year. Calculate the old annual cost and the new annual cost, then compare that difference with how often you actually use the service.

If you use the product frequently, a lower tier or annual plan may still be better value than cancelling. If you rarely use it, the price increase can be a useful trigger to review whether the subscription still belongs in your budget.

Before you cancel or downgrade

1. Check renewal terms

Confirm the next billing date and whether cancellation takes effect immediately or at period end.

2. Compare lower tiers

See whether the features you use are available on a cheaper plan.

3. Check bundles

Removing one service can affect a bundle discount elsewhere, so compare the full account total.

Frequently asked questions

Can a provider raise a subscription price at renewal?

Terms vary by service and jurisdiction. Review the provider's notice, agreement and renewal terms for your account.

Is annual billing always cheaper?

No. Annual plans may offer a discount, but they also require a larger upfront commitment and can have different refund rules.

Can BillSavings AI cancel subscriptions?

No. It helps identify recurring charges and organize what is worth reviewing; cancellation must be completed with the provider or billing platform.
