BILL ERROR DISPUTE TEMPLATE

Billing error dispute letter template

If a charge appears wrong, duplicated, missing a promised credit or does not match the service you expected, document the issue clearly and ask the provider to investigate.

Build a clear dispute message

Enter only non-sensitive details, then generate the message.

Do not enter account numbers, card numbers, passwords or other sensitive information. This is a general communication template, not legal advice.

Keep the dispute factual

Include the bill date, charge description, amount and why you believe it needs review. Save copies of the bill, confirmation emails, chat transcripts or cancellation records that support the request.

Ask for a specific resolution

Request that the company explain the charge, correct the account if appropriate and confirm the result in writing. If a deadline or formal dispute process applies to your account, follow the provider's official instructions.

Start with the right guide

For unfamiliar recurring fees, use Find Hidden Fees. For a sudden total increase, use the monthly bill comparison guide.

Frequently asked questions

What should a billing dispute message include?

Include the charge, amount, statement date, factual reason you believe it needs review, relevant supporting records and the resolution you are requesting.

Should I include my full account or card number?

Avoid putting sensitive payment credentials into a general template. Use the provider's secure account or official dispute channel for required account details.

Does sending a letter guarantee a refund?

No. The provider must review the facts and its own policies or applicable dispute process.
