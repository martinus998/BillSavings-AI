Recurring equipment charge

Equipment rental fee on your bill? Check what you are actually renting

Modems, routers, gateways, receivers and other provider-owned devices can create recurring monthly charges. Before changing anything, identify the device, the monthly amount and the provider's ownership rules.

Review My Bill

Start with the exact device

Modem or gateway

Internet providers may charge for a modem, router or combined gateway. Check whether compatible customer-owned equipment is permitted.

TV receiver or box

Cable and television services may charge per receiver, DVR or additional room device.

Phone or home equipment

Some services include leased hardware, adapters or protection-related devices that appear as a recurring equipment line.

Old equipment still billed

If a device was replaced or returned, compare the return receipt and serial number with the equipment listed on the current statement.

Compare rental cost with alternatives

Multiply the monthly rental amount by twelve to understand the annual cost. Then compare that number with any compatible purchase option, warranty differences, provider support and replacement responsibilities. A cheaper device is not automatically a better choice if it is unsupported or incompatible.

If the fee is on an internet statement, see our internet equipment fee guide. For other recurring extras, use how to find hidden fees on bills and our monthly bill review guide.

Questions to ask the provider

  • What exact device does this rental charge cover?
  • Is the device required for my current plan?
  • Can I use compatible customer-owned equipment?
  • Would changing equipment affect technical support or included features?
  • What is the return process and how do I prove the device was returned?

Frequently asked questions

Is an equipment rental fee always optional?

No. Some providers or plans require certain equipment, while others allow customer-owned alternatives.

Can returning equipment lower the bill immediately?

Not always. The timing of the billing adjustment depends on the provider's cycle and return processing rules.

Should I keep a return receipt?

Yes. Keep the receipt, date and device serial number in case the rental charge continues after the equipment was returned.

Equipment policies, compatibility requirements and return rules vary by provider. BillSavings AI provides informational support and does not guarantee a rental fee can be removed.
