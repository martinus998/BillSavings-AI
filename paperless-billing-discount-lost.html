Paperless billing discount disappeared? Check these settings first

A small paperless-billing discount can vanish after an account, plan or enrollment change. Compare the current bill with an older statement and verify the requirements before assuming the provider made an error.

Review My Bill

What to verify

Paperless status

Confirm that electronic statements are still enabled on the correct account and email address.

Plan eligibility

A plan migration, bundle change or account transfer can sometimes change which discounts apply.

Combined requirements

Some providers require both paperless billing and autopay for the full discount. Check whether either setting changed.

Billing cycle timing

A setting restored near the statement date may not appear until a later billing cycle, depending on provider rules.

Compare the discount line by line

Find the exact discount on an older bill and note the amount, wording and account status at that time. Then compare the current statement. If the discount line disappeared at the same time as a plan or payment change, that timing can help support identify the cause.

If an automatic-payment credit also disappeared, see our lost autopay discount guide.

Questions to ask support

  • Is paperless billing still active on my account?
  • Does my current plan qualify for the same discount?
  • Is autopay also required?
  • When should the discount reappear after enrollment is restored?
  • Did a recent account migration or plan change remove eligibility?

If the bill also added a paper statement charge, review our paper statement fee checklist. For broader bill changes, see how to lower monthly bills.

Frequently asked questions

Will turning paperless billing back on restore the discount immediately?

Not always. Timing and eligibility rules vary by provider and billing cycle.

Can I have paperless billing without autopay?

Often yes, but the discount rules may be different. Check the provider's current requirements.

Can BillSavings AI change my billing settings?

No. It helps you review bill information and identify questions to ask the provider.

Discount rules vary by provider, account and plan. BillSavings AI does not guarantee that a missing discount can be restored.
