What to verify before accepting an offer

  • The exact new monthly total, not just the base plan price.
  • How long a discount or promotional rate lasts.
  • Whether equipment charges change.
  • Whether autopay or paperless-billing requirements apply.
  • Whether a contract, activation fee or other one-time charge is created.
  • What speed, data limit or service level changes with the offer.

Calculate the impact before you call

If the bill jumped recently, use the Monthly Bill Change Calculator. If you are considering paying a third-party negotiation service, use the Bill Negotiation Fee Calculator first.

Why the script uses only real competitor offers

A specific verified offer can help you compare your choices clearly. Inventing an offer can undermine the conversation and is unnecessary. If you do not have a competitor quote, simply ask the provider to review current promotions, loyalty pricing, equipment options and lower-cost plans available on your account.
