Contract fee review

Early termination fee? Check the contract timeline first

Cancellation charges can be confusing because service terms, promotions, equipment and account changes may all overlap. Start by matching the fee to the contract and cancellation dates.

Review My Bill

Four details to verify

Contract end date

Find the stated minimum term and confirm whether cancellation happened before it ended.

Cancellation date

Compare the date you requested cancellation with the provider's recorded effective date.

Promotion terms

Some discounts or credits are tied to keeping service for a specific period.

Equipment obligations

Routers, modems, phones or other equipment may have separate return or payoff requirements.

Do not mix separate charges together

An early termination fee may appear beside a device payoff, final-month charge, non-return fee or tax. Ask the provider to explain each line separately so you know which amount comes from the contract and which comes from equipment or service.

If you are changing providers to save money, compare the final cost of leaving against the expected monthly savings. Our lower monthly bills guide explains why a cheaper headline price may not be cheaper after switching costs.

Questions to ask the provider

  • Which contract clause created this fee?
  • What contract end date do you have on the account?
  • Is any part of this charge an equipment balance?
  • Were there exceptions or cancellation windows in my agreement?
  • Will another final bill or equipment charge arrive later?

Frequently asked questions

Can an early termination fee be waived?

Policies vary by provider and contract. Ask whether the agreement includes any exceptions, but do not assume a waiver is guaranteed.

Should I cancel before comparing the fee?

It is usually useful to understand the contract and switching costs before making a final change.

Can BillSavings AI cancel service for me?

No. It helps organize bill and contract-related charges so you can ask better questions.
