Use the bill itself as your starting point

A useful bill review starts with the statement in front of you. Compare the current and previous total, then identify which line actually changed. For recurring increases, compare the plan price and discounts. For one-time spikes, check setup, upgrade, late-payment, restoration and partial-cycle items. For utilities, compare usage, rate, billing days and meter-reading type.

BillSavings AI can help organize supported documents and highlight line items worth checking, but it does not decide that a charge is wrong and it does not guarantee savings. Provider terms, eligibility and local rules vary.
