Internet promotion check

Your internet promotion ended. Now compare the full price.

Introductory pricing can disappear on a later statement while equipment, add-ons and other charges remain. Before switching providers, identify exactly what discount ended and what the recurring account total will be going forward.

Review My Internet Bill

Start by finding the missing credit

Put an older promotional bill next to the current bill. Look for a credit, discount or lower base rate that no longer appears. If the increase closely matches the missing discount, you have likely found the main change.

If the total increased by more than the expired credit, another line may have changed at the same time. Use our internet bill increase checklist for a broader review.

What can change when a promotion expires

Base monthly rate

A temporary service discount can disappear and the account can move to the provider's regular rate.

Bundle discount

A credit tied to mobile, TV or another service can change separately from the main internet promotion.

Equipment discount

Modem, router or Wi-Fi equipment may have been free or discounted only during the introductory period.

Autopay or paperless credit

A payment-method or paperless discount can disappear for reasons unrelated to the promotion.

Optional add-ons

Streaming, support, security or other services may begin billing after an introductory period.

Taxes and fees

Other bill lines can change independently, so compare the complete statement rather than only the base rate.

Compare the old bill and new bill line by line

Write down the old base rate, promotional credit, equipment charges, add-ons, discounts, taxes and total. Do the same for the new statement. This gives you a simple bridge from the previous total to the new one.

For unfamiliar line items, use our internet bill hidden fees guide. For equipment-specific changes, see internet equipment fees.

Check the original promotion terms

Look for the order confirmation, service agreement or promotional email. Identify the original discount, how long it was scheduled to last and what the account was expected to cost afterward.

This is useful when the provider's current bill label is different from the language used when you signed up.

Ask about current plan options before accepting a new promotion

A replacement promotion may reduce the bill, but it can also change speed, equipment, data terms or other services. Ask for the full recurring price and the regular price after the new promotion ends.

Our how to lower your internet bill guide provides a broader plan-comparison checklist.

Do not compare only the first promotional month

A plan with a very low introductory price can be more expensive later. Compare the discounted period and the expected recurring price afterward, especially if you plan to keep the service beyond the promotion.

Also include any one-time installation or activation costs when comparing a switch.

Before switching providers

  • Confirm the post-promotion price of the current provider.
  • Ask whether a different current plan is available without switching.
  • Compare required equipment charges.
  • Check installation or activation costs at the new provider.
  • Review contract and cancellation terms.
  • Compare data limits or usage rules.
  • Confirm the new provider's regular price after its promotion expires.

A six-step promotion-expiration review

1. Find the old discount

Identify the exact promotional credit or lower rate.

2. Compare every line

Check equipment, add-ons, fees and other discounts too.

3. Read the original terms

Confirm when the promotion was supposed to end.

4. Ask for current options

Compare plans and any new account offers.

5. Compare the long-term total

Include the regular price after a new promotion.

6. Verify the next statement

Make sure the accepted change appears correctly.

Questions to ask your internet provider

  • Which promotion or discount expired?
  • What is my new full recurring monthly total?
  • Did any equipment or add-on pricing change too?
  • What current plans or account offers are available?
  • How long would a replacement promotion last?
  • What will the price be after that promotion ends?
  • Would changing plans affect equipment, speed or data terms?
  • What should the next bill look like after the change?

Frequently asked questions

Can I ask for another internet promotion?

You can ask whether current account offers or plan alternatives are available, but eligibility varies by provider, address and account.

Should I downgrade my speed when a promotion ends?

Only if a lower tier still fits your household usage and the full recurring price is meaningfully lower.

Can an equipment fee start after a promotion?

It can if equipment was previously discounted or included, so compare the equipment line separately.

Should I switch providers immediately?

Not necessarily. Compare the full recurring price, switching costs, equipment and post-promotion pricing first.

Does BillSavings AI negotiate with providers?

No. It helps organize bill changes and questions so you can review the account yourself.
