Sewer bill diagnosis

Why is my sewer bill so high?

A higher wastewater charge may come from water usage, the utility's averaging method, a rate change, a longer billing cycle or a fixed charge. Separate those pieces before assuming the bill is wrong.

Review My Utility Bill

Five things to compare

Water consumption

Many sewer charges are connected to metered water use, so compare gallons or other usage units with the prior period.

Averaging method

Some utilities use a winter average or another historical period rather than current monthly usage.

Rate per unit

Check whether the wastewater or sewer rate changed even if usage stayed similar.

Fixed charges

Service, infrastructure or base charges may appear separately from usage.

Billing days and adjustments

A longer cycle, corrected reading or account adjustment can change the total.

Start with the water side of the bill

If your water consumption rose, the sewer charge may rise with it depending on the utility's billing formula. Review leaks, irrigation, guests and seasonal use using our high water bill checklist.

If water use is nearly unchanged, compare the sewer rate, billing days, fixed charges and any average or adjustment. For a broader utility review, see why a utility bill is too high.

Questions to ask the utility

  • What usage number was used to calculate my sewer charge?
  • Is the bill based on current water use or an average?
  • Did the sewer rate or fixed service charge change?
  • Was there a corrected meter reading or account adjustment?
  • Is there a process for reviewing unusual usage caused by a verified leak?

Frequently asked questions

Can my sewer bill increase even if I used less water this month?

Yes. If the utility uses an average, fixed charges or a prior-period calculation, the sewer amount may not track the current month's water use exactly.

Does outdoor watering always count toward sewer charges?

That depends on the utility's billing method and metering setup. Check the local rules shown on your bill or account.

Can BillSavings AI determine the correct sewer rate?

No. It helps organize the statement so you can compare usage, rates and fees with the utility's published billing method.
