Billing adjustment guide

Prorated charge on your bill? Start with the dates

Proration usually appears when a service, plan or account feature changes in the middle of a billing cycle. The amount often reflects only the days before or after that change.

Review My Bill

Common reasons for a prorated charge

New service started mid-cycle

The first statement may include a partial period plus the next regular billing period, depending on the provider's billing method.

Plan changed

Moving to a different package, speed tier or service level can create partial charges and credits for the days before and after the change.

Add-on added or removed

Equipment, premium features or optional services may be prorated when they are activated or cancelled partway through the cycle.

Service ended

Some providers issue a partial credit, while others bill through the end of a cycle under their terms. Check the cancellation policy before assuming a refund should appear.

How to check the math

Find the billing-cycle start and end dates, then identify the effective date of the service or plan change. Compare the old rate, new rate, any credits and the number of days shown on the statement. A prorated line should make more sense when each period is separated.

If the statement also includes a generic adjustment, fee or one-time charge, compare it with our administrative fee guide and service charge checklist. For a wider review, see how to find hidden fees on bills.

Questions to ask the provider

  • What exact dates does this prorated line cover?
  • What rate was used for each part of the billing cycle?
  • Was a credit applied for the old plan or cancelled service?
  • Does this statement include the next regular billing period too?
  • Will the next bill return to the normal recurring amount?

Frequently asked questions

Is a prorated charge always an extra fee?

No. It is usually a timing adjustment rather than a separate penalty or service fee.

Can a prorated charge be negative?

Yes. A partial-period credit can reduce the bill when a service or price decreases partway through the cycle.

Can BillSavings AI calculate the provider's exact proration rules?

It can help organize the statement, but provider-specific billing rules and contract terms still need to be confirmed with the provider.

Billing calendars and proration rules vary by provider. BillSavings AI provides informational review support and does not determine whether a specific charge is valid.
